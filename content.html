Stumble Guys

Stumble Guys is a multiplayer party game containing 32 players and 3 rounds. Players navigate through various obstacle courses and challenges to be the last one standing. Similar to games like Fall Guys, Stumble Guys only has 3 rounds and is a free-to-play game. This game used to be available on iOS and Android, but now it's available on PC and consoles. The game was developed and published by Kitka Games but was later acquired by Scopely. I played this game since 2021. And this is my journey of winning crowns in Stumble Guys. The video usually contains 10 crowns videos and recorded only in a final round. I also had played and reached final phase in tournament before. Unfortunately, I haven't won till this now. So most videos are just highlights of final phase.
